# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Operators running cold-outbound email at a company using ReplyOS: managing sending domains/mailboxes (AI Cold Mailer) and reviewing/steering AI-drafted replies to inbound leads (AI Auto Responder). They live in this dashboard for extended sessions, scanning tables, charts, and status chips to catch problems (bounces, flagged mailboxes, stalled campaigns) quickly.

## Product Purpose

Replace a third-party cold-email vendor (PlusVibe/Mailscale) with an in-house mailbox/sending/receiving/warmup stack, and give operators a single dashboard to monitor domain health, mailbox warmup, campaign performance, and AI-agent reply activity. Success = an operator can tell at a glance whether sending is healthy and where to intervene.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, technical — infrastructure tooling, not consumer SaaS. Confidence through restraint: clear data, quiet chrome, one accent color used deliberately (status/emphasis), not decoration.

## Anti-references

Not a marketing site. Avoid: gradient hero-metric cards, glassmorphism, bouncy/playful motion, saturated multi-color palettes, generic same-sized icon+heading+text card grids repeated without hierarchy.

## Design Principles

- Data density over decoration — every card earns its space by helping the operator triage.
- Status is legible at a glance — reputation/health states use consistent color + icon language across every page.
- One accent color, used with intent (primary actions, live/active states) — not applied decoratively.
- Consistent module language — dashboard summary cards and their full detail pages (e.g. Health, Campaigns) should visually rhyme, so navigating between them feels like zooming in, not switching apps.
- Tables are a first-class surface, not an afterthought — sortable structure, real hierarchy between header/row/hover states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, status conveyed by icon + label + color (not color alone), visible focus states, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected (already handled globally in styles.css).
